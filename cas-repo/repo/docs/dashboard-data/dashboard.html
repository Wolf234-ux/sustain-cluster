Method and sources

  • India hourly curve: a typical-day model. Its daily average equals the Central Electricity Authority's grid emission factor for FY 2023-24; its shape follows Ember's 2026 data on midday vs evening fossil generation.
  • Tariff: Gujarat HTP-I energy charges for the mid-size billing-demand slab, from the GERC tariff schedule effective April 2026, including its time-of-use peak charge and midday discount. Duty, taxes, demand charges and fuel surcharge are excluded.
  • Great Britain: National Grid ESO Carbon Intensity API forecast, captured live during testing.
  • All results are computed by the open Carbon-Aware Scheduler v2 engine, which logs every forecast, every option scored and every decision.
