*CAS India model. Measured on real Grid-India data: 18:00 → 24%, 00:00 → 23%, 06:00 → 18%, 12:00 → 0% · Gujarat HTP-I energy charge only

This slide answers “does it just delay everything?” No. At noon the job runs immediately, because noon is already clean and cheap. Evening and late-night jobs get the biggest carbon cut. A morning job saves a lot of money because 7 to 11 am is a peak-tariff window in Gujarat.
