AI is turning electricity into the bottleneck

12 GW

India’s data-center capacity by 2030, up from about 2.2 GW in 2025

727 g

CO₂ per kWh on India’s grid, on average — mostly coal

50 GW

More fossil power at 7 pm than at 1 pm, as solar fades and coal ramps up

Much AI work only has to finish by a deadline — yet it runs whenever it was scheduled, often at the dirtiest, priciest hour.

Sources: Wood Mackenzie via Indian Infrastructure (Jul 2026) · CEA CO₂ Baseline Database, FY 2023-24 · Ember H1 2026 via Down To Earth

Three numbers frame the problem. Data-center capacity in India grows more than fivefold by 2030. The grid it plugs into averages 727 grams of CO2 per kWh. And the gap between midday and evening fossil generation is about 50 GW — coal fills the evening.
