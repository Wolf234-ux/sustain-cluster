Carbon: Grid-India hourly data Jan 2024–Jun 2025, scheduled from yesterday’s pattern · Cost: Gujarat HTP-I tariff, energy charge only · 24 h deadline

The headline result. A four-hour, 8 kW job — one GPU server — submitted at 6 pm would run straight into the coal-heavy evening peak and the peak tariff. CAS waits 17 hours and runs it at 11 am the next day. On 539 days of real Grid-India data, that cut the fossil share of the power by about 24% (22–25% depending on assumptions). The energy charge falls 29%, from ₹161.60 to ₹115.20.
