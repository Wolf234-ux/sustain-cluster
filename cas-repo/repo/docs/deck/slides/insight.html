This is the core insight. India’s grid is cleanest at midday because of solar, and dirtiest in the evening when coal ramps up. Gujarat’s tariff discounts exactly the midday hours by 60 paise and surcharges the peaks by 85 paise. So a scheduler that chases clean hours also chases cheap hours.
