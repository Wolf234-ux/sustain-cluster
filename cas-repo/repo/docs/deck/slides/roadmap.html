The plan is staged with two gates. Phase one answers the one open question: do the model’s savings hold on live Indian grid data? We run two or three pilots in Gujarat. If they do, phase two turns the engine into a product that plugs into the schedulers companies already use, with full billing and BRSR reporting. Phase three adds multi-region shifting and demand-response revenue.
