Data: HPE SustainCluster repository (Electricity Maps carbon, Grid-India prices, Open-Meteo weather) · Model: scikit-learn HistGradientBoostingRegressor · 4 h job, 24 h deadline

This answers “is there real ML?” Yes. Two gradient-boosting models forecast the next 48 hours of carbon intensity and power-exchange price for India, trained on public data to the end of 2023 and tested on all of 2024. On prices, scheduling with the ML forecast saved 32.5% on average and captured 94.5% of what perfect hindsight would have saved. Be honest if asked about carbon: the ML’s carbon forecast is only as good as its data, and the public carbon estimates for Western India are too smooth. The pilot will retrain it on measured Indian generation data.
