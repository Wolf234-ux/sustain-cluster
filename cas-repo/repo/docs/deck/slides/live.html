India’s free live data has no forecast, so we proved the engine on Great Britain’s public live forecast. Three deadlines, three behaviours. With 12 hours it runs immediately because waiting would be worse. With 48 hours it finds a windy early Monday and more than halves emissions. The lesson: the more flexibility the customer gives, the more CAS saves.
